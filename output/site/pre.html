Evidence base

This run had no internal corpus. The specified /data sources — VOC, returns, NSRL, NDDC, KAPE, POS, margin, catalog — were not available, so ten domains ran on public sources only and five were cut outright rather than filled in.

The exception is the 2028 priority anchors, taken from the Basketball Apparel ways-of-working deck. Those are internal and they close the single biggest gap: the women's business now has a real base to size against.

Orchestrator B  /  Lead concept

Built on her block

The women's opportunity is not a discovery — Standard Issue is already a named 2028 franchise. The differentiated move inside it is the pattern block itself.

ConsumerFemale basketball fan-participants, 14–28, US-led. Fan-first, court-capable — the evidence says demand here is culture-led, not participation-led.
SilhouetteFull kit system — jersey, short, warm-up layer, base layer — engineered on a women's pattern block rather than a graded-down men's block. Shoulder spec'd 14–15″ against the unisex 17–18″, torso shortened 1–2″, waist-to-hip shaping, short length re-proportioned so the waistband stops oversizing.
Feature setFit geometry is the innovation and the story. Deliberately not a new-material claim — there was no NSRL data in this run to support one.
PriceAccessible core $40–55. Authentic tier $75–90, anchored to the observed $79.99 authentic short. Shelf-anchored only — no elasticity or margin data existed.
PositioningThe category has dressed women in men's patterns for forty years. This is the correction — and it is hard to copy quickly, because it is a pattern-library and grading investment, not a colourway.
RegionUS first; the evidence base is US-centric. Canada attaches free via Toronto Tempo.
SeasonLand against the WNBA season and the NCAA women's tournament. The 2026 two-franchise expansion is a fixed, dated demand event.
ChannelWholesale-led, Dick's-anchored — against recent instinct. Wholesale grew $1.6B last year while Direct fell $1.1B, and Dick's named women's basketball a strength category unprompted.

What would make us wrong

Critical

Margin is completely unknown

A women's-specific block means new patterns, new grading, more SKUs at lower per-SKU volume. It may be structurally dilutive. No cost or margin data existed in this run.

High

Single-athlete concentration

The demand signal leans heavily on one player. Test whether it holds across the 15-team league with Clark-attributable volume stripped out.

High

Cannibalisation unassessed

No catalog data, so overlap with existing women's training and sportswear is unknown. This may substitute volume rather than add it.

Medium

The fit evidence is stale

Pattern-block geometry is a physical constant, but the player complaints behind it date to 2016. Refresh against current returns coding before building.

Medium

Silhouette direction unknown

The culture agent was rejected outright — its sources contradicted each other on baggy versus tailored. We may engineer excellent fit into a silhouette that is moving.

Medium

Every source is public

Outside the 2028 anchors, competitors can read all of this. Timing advantage is close to zero, and adidas is already investing behind basketball.
