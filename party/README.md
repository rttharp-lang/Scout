# SAMPLE SIZE
### A 2-hour party for Nike Basketball Apparel

**Provenance, not product.** Sixty people each bring one pair of their own shoes.
For two hours the room is a lit museum of the team's own taste — sixty-one black
plinths, each with a card the owner filled in themselves. The sixty-first plinth is
lit and empty from the first minute, and nobody explains it until minute 105.
