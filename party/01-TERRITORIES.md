# PHASE 2–3 — SYNTHESIS, TERRITORIES, ELIMINATION

Not an average of eight opinions. Six territories, each built at a point where
independent agents reinforced each other.

---

## T1 · SAMPLE SIZE
**Trade-in door × archive status-scramble × terroir**
Every guest brings one pair of their own shoes. Not new, not product — the pair that
means something. Each goes on a lit plinth with a hand-lettered card: owner, place,
year, one line. Sixty-one plinths. The room is a museum of the team's own taste,
curated by the team, and it fills up as people arrive.
*Sources: Cultural Scout's trade-in door + Experience Architect's status scramble +
Tequila Director's single-estate provenance + Memory Designer's Last Pair.*

## T2 · THE MORGUE FILE
**Killed work × wake × cold-to-warm architecture**
A one-night archive of ideas that died. A rail of tagged poly garment bags, each tag
naming a real project the team lost, submitted anonymously in advance. Cold
fluorescent in the archive, near-black warmth at the bar. At minute 60 the host hangs
one of their own.
*Sources: Creative Director's third territory + Memory Designer's Zeigarnik logic.*

## T3 · PRESS ROW
**The party covered like a game**
Timecode graphics, newsprint, working photographers shooting film, a darkroom in the
back. In the last twenty minutes wet prints go up on a wire and guests pull their own
into a glassine sleeve.
*Sources: Creative Director's first territory + Cultural Scout's tunnel-walk and
broadcast-archive references.*

## T4 · THE LOW POST
**Agave terroir × court geometry**
A candlelit room built inside a full-scale painted free-throw key. Bone, oxblood,
court clay. Hand-thrown clay copitas you keep. A comal. One turntable on a wool rug
at the top of the key.
*Sources: Creative Director's second territory + Tequila Director's regional program.*

## T5 · THE TUNNEL
**NBA tunnel walk × fashion launch**
Arrival is the whole event. A corridor, not a lobby. One photographer on medium
format. No step-and-repeat, no logo wall. Portraits arrive by mail the following week
as a bound set.
*Sources: Cultural Scout signals #10 and #2.*

## T6 · OFF THE RECORD
**The Row's phone ban × the released artifact**
Phones sealed at the door. The night has no digital trace. The only record is a
printed document that arrives on Thursday.
*Sources: Cultural Scout signal #2 + Food + Object's "delay" lever.*

## ELIMINATED

**T6 OFF THE RECORD — 79.** A rule, not a world. It has no visual language of its own,
and confiscating phones from sixty people who work in content is a fight you win on
principle and lose in the room. The good half of it — *the artifact arrives later* —
survives inside any other territory. **Cut.**

**T5 THE TUNNEL — 86.** Culturally the most credible thing here and the least
sustaining: it is a spectacular first ninety seconds attached to ninety-eight minutes
of nothing in particular. It is an *entrance*, not a party. **Cut as a territory,
retained as a component.**

**T4 THE LOW POST — 90.** The best-looking room of the six by a clear margin, and the
only one with no memory anchor in it. Beautiful, warm, and by Thursday morning the
sentence is "it looked amazing," which is the sentence nobody repeats. Also the
literal painted court is one step from the "generic Nike event programming" the host
explicitly ruled out. **Cut.**
